===============================================================================
METHODOLOGY NOTE / INSIGHTS
===============================================================================

Insight candidate (RequiresAuth dual-field):
  Checking RequiresAuth alone is insufficient for AnythingLLM. The correct
  unauthenticated classifier is: RequiresAuth=false AND MultiUserMode=false.
  MultiUserMode=true enforces independent authentication and is NOT captured by
  the RequiresAuth boolean. This explains Cat-07's inflated 71% figure.

Insight candidate (shadow port deception rate):
  51/58 = 87.9% false positive rate on shadow port scan for this corpus.
  The dizquetv catch-all fleet saturates naabu results. Viable discriminator:
  spot-check 3 IPs from any cohort where ALL shadow ports show open simultaneously.
  If they return {"dizquetv":"1.5.3",...}, the entire all-ports-open cohort is decoys.

Insight candidate (managed hosting fingerprint):
  useanything.com = AnythingLLM official managed hosting (Mintplex Labs).
  Subdomain = customer slug. modernlending.useanything.com = Modern Lending customer.
  Managed-service instances can ship auth-off-by-default to end customers.
